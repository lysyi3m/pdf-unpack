# Privacy Policy

**PDF Unpack** — effective 2 October 2026

PDF Unpack does not collect, store or share any personal data. This policy covers the app on Mac,
iPhone and iPad.

- **No network access.** PDF Unpack makes no network requests. On the Mac, the app runs in the
  macOS App Sandbox without network permission, so it cannot send anything off your Mac.
- **Your files stay on your device.** The app reads only the PDFs you open. It writes only where
  you save, or where you drag a file to. Temporary copies for Quick Look, sharing and drag-out live
  in the app's own container. They are deleted when you quit the app on the Mac, and when you next
  open it on iPhone and iPad.
- **No analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.** The app contains no third-party code.
- **Sharing is your choice.** A file leaves your device only if you send it yourself through the
  Share menu, and the service you choose handles it under its own policy.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/lysyi3m/pdf-unpack/issues).
