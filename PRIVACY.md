# Privacy Policy

**PDF Unpack** — effective 23 September 2026

PDF Unpack does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No network access.** The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox without network permission, so it
  cannot send anything off your Mac.
- **Your files stay on your Mac.** The app reads only the PDFs you open. It writes only where you
  save, or where you drag a file to. Temporary copies for Quick Look and drag-out live in the app's
  own container and are deleted when you quit.
- **No analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.** The app contains no third-party code.
- **Sharing is your choice.** A file leaves your Mac only if you send it yourself through the Share
  menu, and the service you choose handles it under its own policy.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/lysyi3m/pdf-unpack/issues).
